## The entry

> One-star review: the ketchup expired in March, the jam has never been opened, and you keep buying more of both.

111 characters, one line. The fridge turns the tables and reviews its owner:
the inventory is the evidence, and the sting is that the owner keeps buying
more of the food nobody ever opens.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is needed:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
